# The Phenomenology of Perception

Merleau-Ponty, M. (1945). Phenomenology of Perception.

> The world is not what I think, but what I live through. I am open to the world, I have no doubt that I am in communication with it, but I do not possess it; it is inexhaustible. (p. xxvii)

## Chapter 1: The Body

> The body is our general medium for having a world. Sometimes it is restricted to the actions necessary for the conservation of life, and accordingly it postures our world around us a biological world; at other times, elaborating upon these primary actions and moving from their literal to a figurative meaning, it manifests through them a core of new significance. (p. 146)
